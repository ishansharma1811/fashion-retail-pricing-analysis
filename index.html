Key Findings

📊

Volume vs. Profit Trade-off

Moving from no discount to 31–40% off raises units per order by 43% but cuts profit per order by 72%. Deeper discounts attract more volume but erode margins significantly.

⚠️

Break-Even at 41%+

Discounts at 41% or deeper yield approximately 1% margin. Footwear, Outerwear, and Activewear actually lose money at these depths.

🎯

Category Elasticity Varies

Accessories and Dresses show strong discount responsiveness (elasticity ~0.9) with high margins (54–47%), making them ideal promotion targets versus Footwear (34% margin, low elasticity).

📉

Seasonal Margin Drag

January and July end-of-season sales run 75% discounted orders, reducing margins to 37% versus 45% in regular months, despite similar revenue levels.

Strategic Recommendations

✓ Cap Footwear & Outerwear Discounts

Limit to 20% maximum. Even 11–20% discounts cut profit per order by 33–41% in these low-margin categories.

✓ Focus on High-Elasticity Categories

Promote Accessories, Dresses, and Tops where volume lift more than offsets margin loss due to higher baseline margins.

✓ Avoid 40%+ Sitewide Promotions

Reserve ultra-deep markdowns for explicit clearance events only. Use bundles and thresholds instead to protect overall margin.
