Key Findings

Discounts grow units but shrink profit

Moving from no discount to 31–40% off raises units per order from 2.2 to 3.1 (+43%), while profit per order falls from $63.8 to $17.8 (-72%).

41%+ discounts are break-even at best

These orders carry roughly 1% margin, and Footwear, Outerwear, and Activewear lose money at that discount depth.

Accessories and Dresses respond best to discounts

They have high margins and strong resale potential, making them the best candidates for targeted promotions.

Seasonal promotions drag profitability

January and July promo periods run ~75% discounted orders and reduce margin to ~37% versus ~45% in normal months.

Recommendations

  • Cap discounts at 20% for Footwear and Outerwear.
  • Avoid 40%+ sitewide promotions except explicit clearance events.
  • Promote Accessories, Dresses, and Tops instead of blanket discounts.
  • Use bundle offers or thresholds instead of broad markdowns.
  • Measure promotions using profit per order, not revenue or units.
