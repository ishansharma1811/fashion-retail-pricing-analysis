Key findings

Discounts increase volume but reduce profit per order.

Moving from no discount to 31–40% off raises units per order from 2.2 to 3.1 (+43%), while profit per order falls from $63.8 to $17.8 (-72%).

Deep markdowns are economically weak.

Orders at 41%+ discounts deliver roughly 1% margin, and categories such as Footwear, Outerwear, and Activewear lose money at that depth.

Best promotional candidates differ by category.

Accessories and Dresses show the strongest discount responsiveness and higher margins, making them better targets for promotions than Footwear and Outerwear.

Risk snapshot

Footwear High risk

Low elasticity and thin margin means discounts erode profitability faster than they help revenue.

Accessories Healthy

Strong discount response and higher margin make it a tactical promotion category.

Seasonal sales Monitor

Jan and Jul promo periods run ~75% discounted orders with margins around 37% versus ~45% in regular months.

Recommended pricing strategy

  • Cap discounts at 20% for Footwear and Outerwear.
  • Avoid 40%+ sitewide markdowns except sealed clearance events.
  • Focus promotions on Accessories, Dresses, and Tops where volume lift offsets margin loss.
  • Use bundles and thresholds instead of blanket cuts to protect margin.
  • Measure success by profit per order rather than revenue or units alone.
